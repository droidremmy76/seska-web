PRIVACY NOTICE

YOUR PROJECT.YOUR INFORMATION.

Updated 17 September 2026. This notice describes this website's current local project-brief tool and optional analytics. Seska Investments Ltd is the business responsible for its use of visitor information.

Your project brief

The form prepares your name, contact details and project requirements in your browser. It does not automatically send them to Seska, upload artwork or save them to a Seska customer database. Copy saves the brief to your device clipboard; Download saves a text file to your device. You decide whether to share it. Do not include sensitive personal information.

Website delivery

The hosting service receives technical information, including your IP address and requests, to deliver and secure the website. Fonts and images are served with the site. Opening a Google Maps link takes you to Google, whose own privacy terms apply.

Optional analytics and recordings

Optional tracking is currently disabled pending account setup. If enabled, Google Analytics measures page visits and Microsoft Clarity provides interaction recordings and heatmaps. Each has a separate, initially unselected choice in Cookie preferences. Neither service loads through this integration before you consent. You can use this website without either service.

Google Analytics may process device and browser information, visit activity and cookie identifiers. Our page-view implementation omits URL queries and fragments and does not intentionally send names, contact details or project briefs. Microsoft Clarity may process device information and page interactions. The project form and brief display are masked from recordings. These services receive network information when you permit them to load.

Google and Microsoft process information under their respective terms and may process it outside your country. See Google's privacy policy and Microsoft's privacy statement. Optional analytics rely on your consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already occurred.

Cookies, storage and changing your choice

Essential local storage named seska.privacy.v1 records your choices, notice version and decision time on this device for up to 180 days. If browser storage is blocked, your choice lasts only for the current page. An expired or invalid choice does not authorize tracking.

If enabled and accepted, Google Analytics uses cookies such as _ga and _ga_*, configured here for up to 180 days. Clarity uses cookies such as _clck and _clsk; provider-side retention and cookie durations must be confirmed before activation and disclosed here. Browser settings may shorten storage periods.

Use Privacy settings in the footer to change your selections or Reject optional to withdraw. Withdrawal stops the enabled integrations, clears their accessible first-party cookies and reloads the page to unload them. It does not automatically delete historical provider records or cookies on another domain. Clearing this site's storage removes your saved preference.

California privacy choices

Where the CCPA applies, you may have rights to know and access, correct and delete personal information, opt out of its sale or sharing, and receive equal service without discrimination. An authorized agent may submit a request subject to appropriate verification. This integration does not enable advertising tracking. The footer's Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information control disables both optional services. Global Privacy Control also disables both, overriding saved opt-ins.

EEA and other privacy rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing. You may withdraw optional tracking consent at any time and complain to your local supervisory authority. The brief tool itself does not transmit your entries to Seska; copies on your own device remain under your control.

Contact and retention

Contact Seska Investments Ltd at the existing business contact location: Nasser Road, Kampala, Uganda, P.O. Box 141411. For privacy questions and access, correction or deletion requests, email info@seskainvestments.com. Please do not send identity documents through the project brief tool.

Before optional services are activated, Seska must confirm hosting log retention, Google and Microsoft retention settings, international transfer arrangements and any additional uses of information received outside this website. This notice must be updated to reflect those confirmed practices.
