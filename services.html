SERVICE 01

Digital & Offset Printing

Business cards, flyers, brochures, books, receipt books and company stationery. Tell us the quantity, size, paper and finish you need.

What is this service for?
Printed business stationery, promotional materials and publications.

What should your brief include?

  • Product, finished size and quantity
  • Paper and finishing requirements
  • Artwork and required deadline

SERVICE 02

Large-Format & Signage

Banners, roll-ups, posters, shop signs and event displays. We help match the material and finishing to where it will be used.

What is this service for?
Banners, displays and signage for shops, events and organisations.

What should your brief include?

  • Display size and quantity
  • Indoor or outdoor use and intended location
  • Material and finishing requirements
Prepare a large-format & signage brief

SERVICE 03

PVC & Proximity ID Cards

Staff and student identification, PVC cards, proximity cards and lanyards. Confirm system compatibility before ordering proximity cards.

What is this service for?
Staff and student identification, with compatibility checks for proximity cards.

What should your brief include?

  • Card type and quantity
  • Artwork and any individual names or details
  • Required system compatibility for proximity cards
Prepare a pvc & proximity id cards brief

Corporate Apparel & Branding

T-shirts, polos, caps and workwear. Discuss embroidery or print, garment sizes, colours and logo placement.

What is this service for?
Garments carrying an organisation’s name, logo or message.

What should your brief include?

  • Garment type, sizes, colours and quantities
  • Logo placement
  • Preferred embroidery or print method

Branded Merchandise

Mugs, notebooks, awards and promotional gifts for your organisation or event. Product availability and branding options are confirmed with your quote.

Print Finishing

Discuss binding, trimming, lamination and finishing options to give your printed work the right feel and durability.

What is this service for?
Binding, trimming and lamination for printed work.

  • Binding, trimming or lamination requirements

Technical Plotting

A0, A1 and A2 drawings for architects, engineers and construction teams. Supply the scale, sheet size and required number of sets.
