What does Seska Investments Ltd do?

Seska Investments Ltd provides printing, design and branding on Nasser Road in Kampala, Uganda. Its services include digital and offset printing, signage, ID cards, corporate apparel, branded merchandise, stickers, print finishing, technical plotting, website design and motion graphics.

Explore the service guide

Where is Seska Investments Ltd located?

Seska Investments Ltd is based on Nasser Road in Kampala, Uganda. The website currently provides an area map; the exact workshop pin is not yet confirmed. Check the contact information before planning your visit.

View business details

What are Seska’s opening hours?

Seska Investments Ltd lists its opening hours as Monday to Saturday, 8:00 am to 6:00 pm. Holiday hours are not listed; confirm availability before a special trip.

View business details

How much does printing at Seska cost?

Seska quotes each job according to product, quantity, size, material and finishing. Delivery may add a separate charge. Share your specifications to request a quote in Ugandan shillings (UGX); the website does not publish a fixed price list.

Prepare your quote details

How long will my printing order take?

Seska confirms turnaround for each order based on the product, quantity, materials and finishing. Include your required deadline in the brief so production and delivery timing can be agreed before the order proceeds.

Understand order approval

Is there a minimum order quantity at Seska?

Minimum quantities at Seska vary by product and production method. State the quantity you need when preparing your brief. Seska can then advise on a suitable option; there is no single published minimum for all services.

Compare service requirements

Can Seska help with artwork and design?

Seska offers design support as well as working with supplied artwork. Explain your project, intended audience and required output. The scope of design work and any design charges are agreed with the quote.

Prepare your artwork

Which artwork file formats does Seska accept?

Seska lists PDF, PSD, JPG, PNG, Word, PowerPoint, AI and EPS as artwork formats. Include the intended finished size. A print-ready PDF is a useful starting point, but suitability must be reviewed for the chosen production process.

Read the artwork guidance

Does Seska deliver outside Kampala?

Delivery from Seska can be arranged across Uganda. Include your destination in the brief so delivery availability, timing and charges can be agreed with your quote. Collection in Kampala is also available.

Plan collection or delivery

Does Seska offer pricing for bulk printing orders?

Seska prices printing according to quantity, material, format and finishing. Request a quote for your required quantity, or ask to compare several quantities. A bulk discount is not automatically guaranteed.

Build a quote brief

Will my printed colours match my screen?

Screens and printed materials can display colours differently. For a Seska print order, share brand colour references and discuss a proof or sample if close colour matching is important. Agree the colour expectations before approving production.

Review before production

How do payment and artwork approval work at Seska?

Seska agrees artwork, specifications, the quote and payment arrangements before production. The website lists Mobile Money, bank transfer and cash; company terms may be available for approved accounts. Confirm payment instructions directly with Seska before paying.

Read the ordering steps

Does the website send my project brief to Seska?

The Seska website prepares a project brief on your device. It does not automatically send the brief or upload artwork. Use Copy or Download to save the brief, then share it through a confirmed Seska contact channel.

Prepare a project brief

How can I contact Seska about privacy or deletion requests?

Email info@seskainvestments.com for privacy questions and access, correction or deletion requests. The project brief tool does not automatically send your form entries to Seska. The privacy notice explains optional analytics and your choices.
