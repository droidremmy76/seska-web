FROM BRIEF TO COLLECTION

How to prepare
your print order.

Start a Seska order with the product, size, quantity, artwork, deadline and delivery destination. Agree the quote and approval before production. The online tool prepares a brief locally; it does not submit an order.

On this page

  1. Prepare the project details
  2. Prepare artwork or describe the design
  3. Save and share your brief
  4. Agree the quote, artwork and payment
  5. Production and finishing
  6. Arrange collection or delivery

  1. STEP 1

    Prepare the project details

    List the product or service, finished size, quantity, preferred materials and finishing, deadline and collection or delivery location. These details help Seska prepare a quote in UGX.

    Check the details needed for your service

  2. STEP 2

    Prepare artwork or describe the design

    Provide ready artwork or explain the design support you need. Seska lists PDF, PSD, JPG, PNG, Word, PowerPoint, AI and EPS. Include the finished size; file suitability is reviewed for the chosen process. The website does not upload these files.

    Read the artwork format answer

  3. STEP 3

    Save and share your brief

    Complete the project brief form, then copy or download the prepared text. It stays on your device until you choose to share it. Direct online enquiry delivery and artwork upload are not connected; use a confirmed Seska contact channel.

    Understand how the brief handles your information

  4. STEP 4

    Agree the quote, artwork and payment

    Confirm specifications, design scope, material, finishing, production timing and payment arrangements with Seska. Share brand colour references and discuss a proof or sample if colour accuracy matters. Production follows the agreed approval.

  5. STEP 5

    Production and finishing

    The approved order moves into the agreed printing, branding or production process. Finishing may include trimming, binding or lamination, according to the job. Timing depends on the agreed product and specifications.

  6. STEP 6

    Arrange collection or delivery

    Collect in Kampala or discuss delivery across Uganda. Agree the destination, availability, delivery charges and timing with your quote. Do not assume a delivery fee or date until it is confirmed.

Ready to describe your project?

The brief tool prepares text on your device. You can copy or download it; it is not automatically sent to Seska.
