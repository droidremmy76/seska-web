ABOUT SESKA INVESTMENTS LTD

Printing, design and branding
on Nasser Road.

Customer Satisfaction Is Our Pride.

Seska Investments Ltd is a printing, design and branding business based on Nasser Road in Kampala. We serve businesses, schools, organisations and individuals who need their ideas made clearly and consistently.

One brief. A connected service.

Our work spans digital and offset printing, signage, identification cards, apparel, merchandise, labels, finishing, technical plotting and digital design. We help you consider the right format, material and finish for the intended use.

Built around your requirements.

Every project begins with a conversation about what you need, how many you need and when you need it. Artwork, specifications, pricing and payment arrangements are agreed before production.

Local service. Wider reach.

Visit us in Kampala, collect your order or arrange delivery across Uganda. Whether you have print-ready artwork or need design support, start by sharing your brief.
