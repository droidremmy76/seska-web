Read all 14 answers about Seska

How much does printing at Seska cost?+

Seska quotes each job according to product, quantity, size, material and finishing. Delivery may add a separate charge. Share your specifications to request a quote in Ugandan shillings (UGX); the website does not publish a fixed price list. Prepare your quote details

How long will my printing order take?+

Seska confirms turnaround for each order based on the product, quantity, materials and finishing. Include your required deadline in the brief so production and delivery timing can be agreed before the order proceeds. Understand order approval

Which artwork file formats does Seska accept?+

Seska lists PDF, PSD, JPG, PNG, Word, PowerPoint, AI and EPS as artwork formats. Include the intended finished size. A print-ready PDF is a useful starting point, but suitability must be reviewed for the chosen production process. Read the artwork guidance

Does Seska deliver outside Kampala?+

Delivery from Seska can be arranged across Uganda. Include your destination in the brief so delivery availability, timing and charges can be agreed with your quote. Collection in Kampala is also available. Plan collection or delivery
